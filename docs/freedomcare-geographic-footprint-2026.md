## 2. New York CDPAP program changes (2025–2026) and how FreedomCare adapted

### 2.1 What the state did

CDPAP itself was **not repealed**. NYSDOH still describes it as the Medicaid program that lets eligible members hire and train their own personal assistant, including a friend or family member, with three hard exclusions: the member’s **spouse**, the member’s **designated representative**, and the **parent of a consumer under 21**. Source: [NYSDOH CDPAP](https://www.health.ny.gov/health_care/medicaid/program/longterm/cdpap/) (page revised May 2026).

What changed is **who may act as fiscal intermediary**:

| Date | Action | Source |
|---|---|---|
| FY 2024–25 enacted budget | Amended Social Services Law **§ 365-f(4-a)** to require the Commissioner of Health to contract with a **single statewide fiscal intermediary (SFI)** | [MLTC Policy 24.04](https://www.health.ny.gov/health_care/medicaid/redesign/mrt90/mltc_policy/2024/24-04.htm) (6 Dec 2024) |
| 17 Jun 2024 | DOH issued **RFP #20524**, New York State Fiscal Intermediary Services | same |
| 27 Sep 2024 | DOH awarded the SFI contract to **Public Partnerships LLC (PPL)** | [Matter of Freedom Care LLC v NYSDOH](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/ny-supreme-court-appellate-division/117848321.html) (reciting the 27 Sep 2024 award) |
| 31 Mar 2025, 11:59 p.m. | All “Current FIs” required to **cease FI services** | [MLTC Policy 24.04](https://www.health.ny.gov/health_care/medicaid/redesign/mrt90/mltc_policy/2024/24-04.htm) |
| **1 Apr 2025** | **PPL is the only entity authorized to provide CDPAP fiscal intermediary services** | [MLTC Policy 24.04](https://www.health.ny.gov/health_care/medicaid/redesign/mrt90/mltc_policy/2024/24-04.htm); [MLTC Policy 25.03](https://www.health.ny.gov/health_care/medicaid/redesign/mrt90/mltc_policy/2025/25-03.htm) (26 Feb 2025); [NYSDOH CDPAP](https://www.health.ny.gov/health_care/medicaid/program/longterm/cdpap/) |
| 1 Mar 2025 onward | Plans must refer **all new** CDPAP consumers to PPL (may also refer to a facilitator) | [MLTC Policy 25.03](https://www.health.ny.gov/health_care/medicaid/redesign/mrt90/mltc_policy/2025/25-03.htm) |
| No later than **1 Apr 2026** | Plans must move existing consumers onto the **revised CDPAP consumer agreement** that requires working with the SFI regardless of facilitator | [MLTC Policy 25.03](https://www.health.ny.gov/health_care/medicaid/redesign/mrt90/mltc_policy/2025/25-03.htm) |
| **1 Sep 2025** | **Minimum Needs Requirements** for new PCS/CDPAS assessments (age 21+): limited assistance with physical maneuvering on **more than two ADLs**, or (dementia/Alzheimer’s) supervision on **more than one ADL**. Grandfathering for people already authorized/enrolled as of 1 Sep 2025 | [NYSDOH CDPAP](https://www.health.ny.gov/health_care/medicaid/program/longterm/cdpap/) |

Facilitators (subcontractors of PPL) may help with enrollment, EVV, and local support. They **do not** do billing, payroll, or benefits — PPL does. Source: [MLTC Policy 25.03](https://www.health.ny.gov/health_care/medicaid/redesign/mrt90/mltc_policy/2025/25-03.htm); official facilitator roster: [pplfirst.com/cdpap-facilitators/](https://pplfirst.com/cdpap-facilitators/). PPL’s page states that **only organizations listed there are authorized facilitators**.

**FreedomCare / Freedom Care / Evergreenchoice do not appear on PPL’s facilitator list** (checked 18 Aug 2026).

### 2.2 How FreedomCare adapted

FreedomCare’s New York business was built as a **CDPAP fiscal intermediary**. After the SFI statute, it:

1. **Bid for the SFI contract and lost to PPL** (award 27 Sep 2024).  
2. **Sued to unwind the award** (`Freedom Care LLC v. New York State Department of Health`, Index No. 161036/2024). Supreme Court, New York County (Saunders, J.) denied the petition and dismissed the Article 78 on **27 Mar 2025** ([2025 NY Slip Op 31018(U)](https://law.justia.com/cases/new-york/other-courts/2025/2025-ny-slip-op-31018-u.html)). The Appellate Division, First Department **affirmed on 21 Oct 2025** ([FindLaw recitation](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/ny-supreme-court-appellate-division/117848321.html); also reported as 242 A.D.3d 573 (2025)). The court held DOH performed a meaningful RFP review and that PPL’s regional subcontractors met SSL § 365-f(4-a).  
3. **Stopped being a CDPAP FI on 1 Apr 2025**, because the statute left no residual FI license for losers.  
4. **Did not become a PPL facilitator** (absent from [PPL’s roster](https://pplfirst.com/cdpap-facilitators/)).  
5. **Pivoted the New York consumer brand to LHCSA Personal Care Services**: certified PCA/HHA employees, plan authorization, nurse visits, matching or “bring your own aide” **if the aide is not a spouse, parent, son, son-in-law, daughter, or daughter-in-law**. Sources: [freedomcare.com/new-york/](https://freedomcare.com/new-york/); [freedomcare.com/faqs/new-york/](https://freedomcare.com/faqs/new-york/) (“Even though FreedomCare no longer works with CDPAP in New York, you can still have a family member or friend become your paid home aide through our LHCSA model.”).  
6. **Ran a CDPAP→LHCSA conversion funnel**: free PCA certification, sign-on bonus, “hassle-free” transition, eligibility gated on 3+ months with the patient, **not living with the patient**, and the close-relative bar. Source: [freedomcare.com/lhcsa/](https://freedomcare.com/lhcsa/).  
7. **Expanded the LHCSA geographic license**, not the FI role: Albany site opened **26 Nov 2024** (certificate 1957L004) and Suffern/Rockland site opened **15 May 2026** (certificate 1957L003), in addition to the 2014 Jamaica/NYC license (1957L001). Source: NYSDOH HFIS via [Health Facility General Information](https://health.data.ny.gov/Health/Health-Facility-General-Information/vn5v-hh5r) (see §4).

NYSDOH simultaneously warned the market that **pushing CDPAP consumers into LHCSAs with false comparisons is unlawful**. On **11 Mar 2025** DOH issued cease-and-desist letters to certain FIs and LHCSAs for, among other things: telling PAs they can provide PCS to a family member in violation of **18 NYCRR 505.14**; telling people CDPAP ends on 1 Apr 2025; saying a LHCSA aide can do the same work as a CDPAP PA; charging fees for the 40-hour PCA training. Source: [NYSDOH press release, 11 Mar 2025](https://health.ny.gov/press/releases/2025/2025-03-11_cdpap.htm). The 20 Mar 2025 update said ~45,000 consumers were moving CDPAP → PCS. Source: [NYSDOH 20 Mar 2025 update](https://www.health.ny.gov/press/releases/2025/03-20-2025_cdpap_update.htm). The public letters do not name FreedomCare; they describe the exact conversion narrative FreedomCare’s LHCSA pages use, which is why the **legal differences in §3** matter.

NY FAQ still contains a leftover CDPAP upsell (“ask FreedomCare whether New York’s CDPAP program may be a better option”) that contradicts the later FAQ that FreedomCare no longer works with CDPAP. Treat the **NY state page + “no longer works with CDPAP” FAQ** as the current product, not the leftover sentence.

## 3. LHCSA vs FI designation

These are **different legal statuses** under New York law. FreedomCare held both historically (FI for CDPAP; LHCSA for PCS). After 1 Apr 2025 it can hold only the LHCSA in New York.

| | **Fiscal intermediary (FI / SFI)** | **Licensed Home Care Services Agency (LHCSA)** |
|---|---|---|
| Statute / rules | SSL **§ 365-f**; **18 NYCRR 505.28** | Public Health Law **Article 36**; LHCSA licensure (PHL § 3605 / § 3605-a enforcement) |
| Program | **CDPAP / CDPAS** only | **Personal Care Services (PCS)** and related home care (HHA, homemaker, etc.) |
| Who is the employer | Consumer is the employer (joint-employer structure with the FI for payroll). FI processes wages, tax withholdings, employment records | **Agency employs** the aide. Consumer does not jointly employ |
| Who may be the worker | Friend or family, **except** spouse, designated representative, parent of a consumer under 21. **No PCA/HHA certificate required** | Certified **PCA or HHA**. **18 NYCRR 505.14** restricts PCS by family in ways CDPAP does not. FreedomCare’s own rule: cannot be spouse, parent, son, son-in-law, daughter, or daughter-in-law; LHCSA transition page also requires living separately |
| Training / background | Consumer trains the PA. FI handles payroll/compliance. No LHCSA-style 40-hour PCA course as a condition of CDPAP | State-approved PCA/HHA training, competency, health screening, **NYSDOH fingerprint background check** |
| Who authorizes hours | LDSS or Medicaid managed care / MLTC, then FI pays against that authorization | Same assessment/authorization path, but the **plan contracts with the LHCSA**, not with PPL |
| Who may operate (2026) | **Only PPL**, plus optional **facilitators** that cannot payroll | Any Article 36 LHCSA with a live operating certificate |
| FreedomCare (2026) | **No** — lost SFI bid; not on PPL facilitator list | **Yes** — Evergreenchoice, LLC d/b/a Freedomcare, three HFIS sites (see §4) |

NYSDOH’s own CDPAP page tells consumers to read the Department’s CDPAP-vs-PCS comparison before choosing. Source: [NYSDOH CDPAP — “Understanding the Differences Between CDPAP and Personal Care Services (PCS)”](https://www.health.ny.gov/health_care/medicaid/program/longterm/cdpap/).

**Outside New York**, “FI” is not a New York statutory title. FreedomCare uses FI-like language in **Illinois HSP** and **Nevada PCS** FAQs (payroll intermediary). In **Colorado** it is explicit that it is **IHSS / traditional agency, not CDASS FMS**. In **Georgia, Indiana, Connecticut, Massachusetts** the live-in **structured family caregiving / adult foster / AFL** model is an **agency stipend** product, not consumer-employer CDPAP.

## 5. Expansion pattern and remaining white space

### 5.1 Pattern

1. **2014–2016: New York LHCSA + CDPAP FI.** HFIS open date 11 Feb 2014 (Evergreenchoice LHCSA). NPI `FREEDOM CARE LLC` 10 Feb 2016. Headquarters remains Long Island (Lake Success). A 2018 WUSA9 piece already described FreedomCare as managing thousands of New York CDPAP cases ([WUSA9](https://www.wusa9.com/article/news/nation-world/new-program-pays-family-or-friends-to-be-caregivers/507-a377ffb6-7153-4839-b1f7-b36d7b0cc2b2)).  
2. **2019–2021: first non-NY consumer-directed personal care markets** that look like CDPAP (member chooses the aide; FreedomCare payrolls): **Missouri CDS** (NPI 2019), **Nevada PCS** (2020), **Pennsylvania PAS / CHC** (2021), **Arizona ALTCS** (2021).  
3. **2022–2024: live-in stipend / structured family caregiving belt**, which does not require a CDPAP-style FI statute: **Colorado IHSS** (2022), **Indiana SFC** (2023), **Connecticut AFL** (2023), **Massachusetts AFC** (2023), **Georgia SFC** (2021 entity / later page), **Oregon IHC** (2023). LinkedIn for former launch lead David Silverberg describes taking FreedomCare from one state to ten by mid-2024.  
4. **2025: Midwest agency/HSP wave after the NY FI cliff.** Same-week EIN press launches on **24 Jun 2025** for **Illinois HSP**, **Michigan Home Help**, and **Ohio PCS/SFC** ([Illinois](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/824075206/freedomcare-expands-home-care-services-in-illinois-through-the-medicaid-home-services-program); [Michigan](https://www.thedailywolverine.com/article/824073590-freedomcare-launches-medicaid-in-home-care-and-personal-support-through-michigan-s-home-help-program); [Ohio](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/823463104/freedomcare-expands-in-home-care-with-medicaid-personal-care-services-and-structured-family-caregiving-in-ohio)). Wisconsin is claimed on the 2026 homepage but is not in the states sitemap.  
5. **2025–2026 NY: LHCSA geography, not FI recovery.** Albany LHCSA Nov 2024; Suffern LHCSA May 2026. CDPAP volume was replaced by certified-aide PCS, with a conversion campaign that NYSDOH was actively policing industry-wide.  
6. **Shell NPIs ahead of public launch.** Entities exist for **CA, TX, NC, MN, RI, SD, KS, NH, MT, ME** while those states are still off the 15-state list. That is the observable pipeline.

Product pattern, not geography: enter where (a) Medicaid will pay a **family/friend aide** through an agency-with-choice or vendor-FI, or (b) a **live-in structured family caregiving / adult foster / AFL** per-diem exists. Avoid markets whose FMS is a state monopoly or a closed vendor panel unless they can enroll as the **agency** side (Colorado IHSS vs CDASS; New York LHCSA vs PPL).

### 5.2 Remaining white space

**Federal baseline.** CMS: every state may offer self-direction under 1915(c), 1915(i), 1915(j), and/or 1915(k). Source: [Medicaid.gov — Self-Directed Services](https://www.medicaid.gov/medicaid/long-term-services-supports/self-directed-services).

**State baseline (2025 survey, published 5 Jan 2026).** KFF: of 50 responding states, **Alaska is the only one that reported no self-direction** in any home-care program. **All responding states pay family caregivers in at least some circumstances.** Florida did not respond. Source: [KFF, Medicaid’s Home Care Support for Family Caregivers in 2025](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/medicaids-home-care-support-for-family-caregivers-in-2025/).

So “states with self-directed Medicaid that FreedomCare does not cover” is **almost the entire remainder of the map**, not a short list. FreedomCare’s 15 claimed states leave **35 states + DC** (and, on KFF’s survey, Alaska has no self-direction to cover).

**Uncovered states and DC (FreedomCare not on the 15-state list):**  
AL, AK, AR, CA, DE, DC, FL, HI, ID, IA, KS, KY, LA, ME, MD, MN, MS, MT, NE, NH, NJ, NM, NC, ND, OK, RI, SC, SD, TN, TX, UT, VT, VA, WA, WV, WY.
