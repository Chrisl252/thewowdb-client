    How these WoW deals work

    This finder scans the latest Auction House snapshot for your realm (and region-wide commodities) and surfaces listings priced below where they should be. Every row shows the current asking price, a reference price, and an estimated profit so you can judge whether a flip is actually worth it.

    Under market
    The cheapest listing is well below the item's typical market price. Buy it and relist closer to market for the difference.
    Below vendor
    The item is listed for less than a vendor will pay you for it. Buy it, sell it to a vendor, and pocket guaranteed gold.
    High spread
    There's a large gap between the cheapest listing and the next one up. Buy the cheap one and relist just under the next seller.

    Estimated profit is a guide based on the latest snapshot, not a guaranteed sale. Under-market profits are shown after the 5% AH sale fee.
