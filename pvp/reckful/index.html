The tribute

Reckful is a human rogue trainer in the Cathedral of Light. Blizzard placed him there after the community filled that hall in July 2020. Talk to him, tell him it was good to see him, or /hug — he hugs back.

He will not train Assassination rogues. That line is the old arena joke: he climbed the ladder as Subtlety when Assassination was the easy queue.

Pin source: Wowhead NPC 173819 mapper (Stormwind zone 1519, cluster 51.4–51.6 / 44.4–44.6) and Wowpedia’s Cathedral listing 51.6, 44.38. Displayed waypoint is the cluster center: 51.6, 44.4. No coordinates were invented for this page.
